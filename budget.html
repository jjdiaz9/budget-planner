Mixing notes

  • Moisten the base mix first — peat is hydrophobic when dry and mixes far better damp.
  • Blend the three meals together, then fold into the mix and compost in layers so the amendments distribute evenly.
  • This provides all the nutrition seedlings need for 6–8 weeks; no extra fertilizer required.
  • Store leftovers dry until you need them.
  • A white mold-like bloom on the surface is Actinomycetes — beneficial, not a problem.
  • For larger containers, swap the seed-starting mix for a coarser potting soil as the base.
  • Weights are rough estimates from typical bulk densities — use the volume measures as the source of truth.
